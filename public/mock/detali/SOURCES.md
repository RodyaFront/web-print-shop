## Resin color psychology (`--resin-*` in `main.css`)

| Material | Token | Hue idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Стандартний | `--resin-standard` `#8b939e` | Calm “as-printed” cool grey |
| Інженерний | `--resin-engineering` `#2a564c` | Heavy industrial teal / strength |
| Ливарний | `--resin-castable` `#6b3485` | Wax / jewelry casting purple |

Wave filters and titles both target the same token.
